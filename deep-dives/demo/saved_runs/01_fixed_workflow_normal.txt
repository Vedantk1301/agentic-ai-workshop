==========================================
PART B: fixed workflow  (scenario: normal)
==========================================
Question: Find vegetarian lunch under Rs 80, and tell me whether the canteen is still open.

  1. read request      -> {'budget': 80, 'vegetarian': True, 'wants_hours': True, 'wants_refund': False}
  2. validate          -> budget Rs 80, vegetarian=True
  3. search menu       -> 1 match(es): [{'name': 'Veg sandwich', 'price': 60}]
  4. check hours       -> open_now=True at 12:30
  5. present answer

ANSWER: In stock vegetarian options under Rs 80: Veg sandwich (Rs 60). The canteen is open now (it's 12:30) and closes at 16:00.
